# Interim Report — EDA Insights

## Key Insights (preliminary)

1. Account ownership increased from 46% (2021) to 49% (2024) — modest growth despite rapid mobile money expansion.
2. Mobile subscriber base (~65M) suggests broad reach for mobile financial services; however, registered accounts vs. active usage gap persists.
3. 4G coverage (~45%) and agent density (~8 agents/100k) indicate infrastructure constraints in rural areas.
4. Events (Telebirr, M-Pesa) align temporally with upticks in digital payment indicators; causal attribution requires modeling.
5. Data gaps: gender- and region-disaggregated Findex microdata and operator-level active account figures limit precision.
